      Spotlight plan

      The gym lights stay off, so every display is lit by its own spotlight. This is what the lighting team asked each section for: where each light goes and how many we need. All spots are warm 3000K clamp lamps on the wall tops at 8′-9″, on 2 ft arms, the same fixture as Mulyo so they can be ordered together. Numbers match the plan below and the labels in the 3D view (turn on Exhibit lighting).

      Spotlight schedule

      • Mount positions are measured along the wall top: north and south walls from the east (front door) end, room dividers and end walls from the north wall. Aim each light at the point listed, then fine-tune on site.

      Space check

      For each room: what sits on the right and left as you walk in, how much floor the people using it need, and the walkway left between them. Numbers are the tightest point in each room.

      5′-0″ or more two people pass comfortably 3′-6″ to 5′-0″ fine for one-way flow under 3′-6″ pinch point, people queue in the walkway
      • Assumed depths: a person at a table, counter or tablet needs 2′-6″; someone browsing the wardrobe 3′-0″; devotees offering petals 3′-0″; people reading a panel need a 1′-6″ band; volunteers serving from behind a table need 2′-0″.
      • ADA requires at least 3′-0″ of clear route; 5′-0″ lets two people pass or a wheelchair turn. Every doorway is 5′-0″ wide.
      • Walls are 8′-0″ tall. Panels hang from 1′-6″ to 7′-6″, and header banners sit above the walls from 8′-3″ to 10′-0″. Adult eye height is about 5′-3″; a child’s is about 3′-9″.

      Notes before you build

      • No open flames. LED diyas at the front door and flower petals at the ghar mandir. String lights should be UL-listed and plugged into switched power strips. Ask the venue and fire marshal what they require (flame-retardant plywood or FR paint, clear exits, exit signs and egress lights left on).
      • Freestanding walls. Built the same way as Mulyo. The outer walls stand on jacks with sandbags. Each room divider bolts to the north and south walls with L-brackets and stands on T-feet at the doorway ends. The door façade adds weight to the east wall, so brace both sides of the front door.
      • Outside the footprint. The rangoli, greeters, shoe rack and lights L01–L03 use an apron of about 4 ft on the main hall side of the front door. Confirm that space is free with the layout team.
      • Food safety. Only pre-packed sweets and snacks, with allergen labels. The tasting team wears gloves and keeps a bin at the dining room exit. Check with the mandir’s food-safety coordinator.
      • Ghar mandir. Coordinate the mandir, murtis and decoration with the women’s overall centrepiece work. Collect offered petals respectfully at the end of each day and refresh them every morning.
      • Power. Outlets are needed for the 3 tablets (living room south wall), the bhajan speaker and string lights (ghar mandir), and the spotlights on every wall. Tape every cord down at the wall base so nothing crosses the walkway.
      • Still to confirm. The Diwali phrase for the listening wall and the 8–10 languages and voices to record it. Mehndi booth yes or no. Gujarati and music class details for the brochures. Who lends the sofa, wardrobe, tables and instruments, with names on every borrowed item.
